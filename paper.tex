\begin{abstract}
Standard General Relativity formulates cosmological dynamics within a $(3+1)$-dimensional pseudo-Riemannian manifold under the invariance of the speed of light ($c$). However, it leaves quantum superposition, non-local entanglement, the geometric origin of rest mass, and measurement-induced state reduction unresolved. In this paper, we construct a mathematically consistent $(3+3)$-dimensional spacetime framework comprising three spatial coordinates ($\mathbf{r} \in \mathbb{R}^3$) and three orthogonal temporal dimensions ($\mathbf{t} = (t_1, t_2, t_3) \in \mathbb{R}^3$). By enforcing an extended $Sp(2,\mathbb{R}) \times U(1)$ gauge symmetry, we eliminate negative-norm ghost states inherent in multi-time formulations and preserve $S$-matrix unitarity. Applying a Wick Rotation to the internal temporal axis ($t_2 \to -i \tau_2$), we derive rest mass $m_0$ as the positive-definite intrinsic oscillation energy ($E_2 = m_0 c^2$) of system photons ($\gamma_{sys}$) without introducing tachyonic instabilities. Furthermore, we redefine the thermodynamic arrow via the asymmetric projection of microstate phase-space densities $\rho$, resolving the $SO(3,3)$ kinematic symmetry paradox. Crucially, we delineate static $1/r^2$ gravitational potential fields from dynamic transverse quadrupolar gravitational radiation, reconciling Birkhoff's Theorem with LIGO observations. Testable predictions for attosecond phase corrections and fundamental qubit dephasing limits are also provided.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Extended Gauge Formalism}

The mathematical incompatibility between Quantum Mechanics (QM) and General Relativity (GR) stems from the ontological nature of measurement, spatial geometry, and intrinsic mass. While GR treats spacetime as a classical $(3+1)$-dimensional continuum, QM relies on non-unitary wavefunction collapse and scalar rest masses defined in an abstract Hilbert space.

To construct a mathematically self-consistent geometry, we define a six-dimensional pseudo-Riemannian manifold $\mathcal{M}^6$ equipped with dimensionless scale factors $(\alpha_2, \alpha_3)$:
\begin{equation}
ds^2 = dx^2 + dy^2 + dz^2 - c^2 dt_1^2 - \alpha_2^2 c^2 dt_2^2 - \alpha_3^2 c^2 dt_3^2 = g_{MN} dX^M dX^N
\end{equation}
where $X^M = (\mathbf{r}, c t_1, \alpha_2 c t_2, \alpha_3 c t_3)$. In macroscopic domains, $\alpha_2, \alpha_3 \sim \ell_P / \lambda_C \ll 1$ (where $\ell_P$ is Planck length and $\lambda_C$ is Compton wavelength), ensuring a smooth low-energy reduction to the classical $(3+1)$-dimensional Minkowski metric.

\subsection{Axiom of Light-Speed Invariance and Null Geodesics}
In $\mathcal{M}^6$, the \textbf{Constancy of the Speed of Light} remains the unbroken physical invariant. For any fundamental field propagating along a Null Geodesic ($ds^2 = 0$), the geometric line element yields:
\begin{equation}
dr^2 = c^2 dt_1^2 + \alpha_2^2 c^2 dt_2^2 + \alpha_3^2 c^2 dt_3^2 = c^2 \|\mathbf{d\tau}_{\text{temporal}}\|^2
\end{equation}
Equation (2) establishes that spatial displacement is strictly bounded by the norm of the temporal vector, forbidding tachyonic signals and closed timelike curves (CTCs).

\subsection{Elimination of Ghost States via $Sp(2,\mathbb{R}) \times U(1)$ Gauge Symmetry}
Multi-time geometries naturally introduce negative-norm physical states (ghosts) due to additional timelike metric signatures. To enforce strict unitarity, we extend the two-time physics gauge constraints by imposing an $Sp(2,\mathbb{R}) \times U(1)$ gauge algebra over the phase-space operators $(\hat{X}^M, \hat{P}^M)$. Physical state vectors $|\Psi_{\text{phys}}\rangle$ are required to satisfy the system of first-class constraints:
\begin{equation}
\hat{H}_0 |\Psi_{\text{phys}}\rangle = \eta_{MN} \hat{P}^M \hat{P}^N |\Psi_{\text{phys}}\rangle = 0
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\hat{H}_2 |\Psi_{\text{phys}}\rangle = (\hat{P}_2^2 + \hat{X}_2^2) |\Psi_{\text{phys}}\rangle = 0, \quad \hat{H}_3 |\Psi_{\text{phys}}\rangle = (\hat{P}_3^2 + \hat{X}_3^2) |\Psi_{\text{phys}}\rangle = 0
\end{equation}
These gauge conditions decouple the unphysical timelike degrees of freedom along $t_2$ and $t_3$, ensuring a positive-definite norm in the physical Hilbert space and guaranteeing strict $S$-matrix unitarity.

\subsection{Phase-Space Density Projection Vector}

\begin{equation}

\begin{equation}
\frac{\delta S_{\text{ensemble}}}{\delta t_1} \gg 0, \quad \left\langle \frac{\delta S_{\text{ensemble}}}{\delta t_2} \right\rangle_{\text{macro}} = 0, \quad \left\langle \frac{\delta S_{\text{ensemble}}}{\delta t_3} \right\rangle_{\text{macro}} = 0
